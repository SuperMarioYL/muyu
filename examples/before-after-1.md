# Example 1 — Instagram travel post (小红书博主转 IG)

## Before (score: see `muyu score`)

Dear friends, today I want to share my trip in Kyoto. Although it was raining, but the temple was still very beautiful. I very like the quiet streets in the morning. Everyday I took many photos there. The price of the lunch is very cheap, only 500 yen. Have you ever wondered why Japan keeps its old city so well? In today's fast-paced world, it is important to slow down. This trip helps me broaden my eyes. I will come back again. Wish you a good day!

## After

Rain in Kyoto, and the temples somehow get better. I kept drifting back to these quiet morning streets — camera out every day, lunch for 500 yen. Ever wonder how the old city survived intact? Slowing down is the whole point. It changed how I see my own neighborhood back home. Already planning the return trip.

## Notes

- 公函开场（Dear friends）→ 直接进场景
- 虽然…但是 → Although 与 but 只留一个
- broaden my eyes → broaden my horizons
- Everyday → Every day；wish you a good day → have a good one / 直接收尾
