# Example 2 — Substack essay (公众号作者转 Substack)

## Before (score: see `muyu score`)

As we all know, English writing is important for creators. In today's fast-paced world, many Chinese creators want to share their stories with the world. However, their English posts always have some problems. First, the collocation is not correct. Moreover, the tone is too formal. Furthermore, the AI-generated text can be easily recognized. It's not just a language problem, it's a cultural problem. Please kindly let me share three tips with you. Although these tips are simple, but they are very useful. In conclusion, if you want to improve your English writing, you should practice every day and read more native content. I sincerely hope that this article can help you. Thank you for your understanding.

## After

Most creators I know can read English fine — their drafts still give them away within two sentences. The tells are specific: collocations carried over from Chinese, a formal register that belongs in an email to a professor, and the flat cadence every AI draft shares. It's a culture problem more than a grammar problem. Three fixes that survived my last year of posting: read what native writers actually publish (not textbooks), rewrite your own old drafts instead of studying rules, and read every sentence aloud before it ships. Simple, not easy. That's the practice.
